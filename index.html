Biography

Chasing Reverbs is the solo project of Martin Eibisch, a composer and producer of instrumental music based in Cologne, Germany. What started as a pandemic-era creative outlet in February 2021 has grown into a catalog of over 60 releases — soothing guitar and piano instrumentals designed as a sonic escape from the noise of everyday life.

With over 60,000 monthly Spotify listeners and more than 2 million streams per year, Chasing Reverbs has built a dedicated audience entirely through independent means — no major label, no live performances, no viral moments. Just consistent, handcrafted music released every six to eight weeks.

Since 2023, Chasing Reverbs has partnered with Sky Valley Records, a small independent label dedicated to instrumental guitar music, for EP releases and playlist campaigns. A vinyl edition of the latest album is available via ElasticStage.

“There’s enough buzz, noise, and fake news out there. We don’t need words. Let’s be quiet for a second — and rest. This is the sound before the silence.”
